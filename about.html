Academic Faculty & Systems Architect

Engineering Solutions with Purpose and Rigor.

I am Antonio Jr. L. Piloton, an Information Technology educator, systems developer, and IT consultant serving as an Assistant Professor at Agusan del Sur State University (ADSSU). My practice spans computing instruction, enterprise software engineering, relational database architecture, and applied research.

I graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) from Surigao State College of Technology (now Surigao del Norte State University - SNSU) in 2020. I subsequently completed my Master in Information Technology (MIT) from SNSU in 2023, where my research and system deployment earned the Best Capstone Project Award.

I have completed all academic requirements (CAR) in the Doctor of Information Technology (DIT) program at St. Paul University Philippines (SPUP) in Tuguegarao City. As an active computing educator and practitioner, I bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world system architecture—building full-stack web platforms, offline-first mobile apps, and robust database backends for commercial and institutional environments.
