SCVAX: A solution to nationwide vaccination management information system

R. A. Reyna, A. C. C. Reyna, D. C. Mangca, J. M. Gallera, P. R. Ruaya Jr., G. B. Galila, R. A. C. Cabañero, A. L. Piloton Jr., A. S. Salvador, and G. Z. Gamboa Jr.

Journal of Engineering Science and Technology (JESTEC), Vol. 18 (Special Issue on ICITE 2023), December 2023, pp. 108–123.

Abstract: Introduces SCVax, an application platform engineered to optimize and streamline nationwide vaccination programs. Highlights functionalities including automated mapping, data visualization, robust data handling, and QR code integration to ensure data reliability and confidentiality.

Development of Records Tracking Management System with QR Code

Antonio Jr. L. Piloton, Gregorio Z. Gamboa Jr., Monalee A. Dela Cerna, and Ritchie A. Reyna
