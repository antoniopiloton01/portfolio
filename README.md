# Antonio Jr. L. Piloton, MIT — Software Developer & IT Consultant Portfolio

A clean, multi-page professional website for **Antonio Jr. L. Piloton, MIT** — Software Developer, IT Consultant, Systems Architect, and Assistant Professor III at **Agusan del Sur State University (ADSSU)**.

Designed with an authentic, human-crafted editorial aesthetic (Deep Slate `#0F172A`, Classic Blue `#1D4ED8`, and Warm Off-White `#FAFAFA`) without generic AI templates, neon glowing blobs, or artificial animations.

---

## 🧭 Multi-Page Navigation (Real URLs — No '#' Hashes)

Each tab in the navigation bar routes directly to its own dedicated page:

* **[index.html](index.html):** Executive overview, core capabilities, and selected featured systems.
* **[about.html](about.html):** Professional biography, academic background (DIT CAR, MIT Best Capstone Awardee, BSIT), certifications, and teaching practice.
* **[services.html](services.html):** Custom business POS systems, Laravel web applications, offline-first React Native mobile apps, and graduate research technical consultation.
* **[projects.html](projects.html):** Real-world software systems (RetailPOS, BantayEskwela, Examora, Records Tracking System, SCVAX, and UniLib SIS) with technical stack and repository links.
* **[research.html](research.html):** Peer-reviewed research publications, including Scopus-indexed *SCVAX* in JESTEC and *Records Tracking Management System* in IJFMR.
* **[contact.html](contact.html):** Dedicated inquiry form, consultation coordinates, and direct email connection (`antoniojrpiloton@gmail.com`).

## 🎨 Visual Design & Color Palette

* **Canvas Background:** Warm Paper Off-White (`#FAFAFA`) / Surface (`#FFFFFF`)
* **Headings & Primary Text:** Deep Slate (`#0F172A`)
* **Body Text:** Muted Slate (`#475569`)
* **Interactive Elements:** Classic Brand Blue (`#1D4ED8`, `#1E40AF`)
* **Hairline Borders:** Refined Slate (`#E2E8F0`)
* **Typography:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` for structured headings, `Inter` for body reading comfort, and `JetBrains Mono` for monospace specifications.
* **Strict Anti-AI Aesthetic:** Clean layouts, natural contrast, authentic case studies, zero glowing neon gradients, and zero fake decorative elements.
